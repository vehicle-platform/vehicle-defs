**Split perfboards (`Board A / B / C1 / C2`), Standoffs, IP67 Enclosures, EMI Shielding & Tools:**

> **Design rule (D-082):** Never cover `NUCLEO-H755ZI-Q` (`133.3×70 mm`) with a single monolithic `9×15 cm` perfboard (it flexes like a drumhead under vibration, couples 12 V switching noise into analog/safety circuits, blocks the `ST-LINK` USB/LEDs, and prevents unplugging the Scale 1B sensors). Instead, build the 2nd story inside the `170×120×55 mm` Main Box from two side-by-side double-sided epoxy perfboards (A, B) and keep the two IMU boards (C1, C2) out of the stack in their own frame-mounted boxes:
> 1. **`Board A` (`5×7 cm` — core power & CAN, Scale 1A):** `1N5822` reverse-polarity Schottky, `SMBJ30CA` TVS, LC stage (`L1`), `HW-788` / `LM2596HVS` 5 V buck, `INA226`, `1 kHz` battery divider, `SN65HVD230 ×2`, `microSD`. Bench-tested at 12–14 V (**Gate B1**) before it is plugged onto the Nucleo.
> 2. **`Board B` (`7×9 cm` — Scale 1B QM conditioners, detachable):** `MAX31856` EGT amp, `PC817` optocouplers for the 12 V switch taps (clutch, brake light, fan; the M7 `TAP_*` channels), `3× 10K NTC` dividers, spark-pickup input (`1N4148` + 3.3 V Zener + `74HC14`), `LJ12A3` level shifter. **Board B holds no safety input.** Unplugging it returns the hardware to **Scale 1A (CAN-only)**.
> 3. **`Board C1` = `C1-IO` + `C1-IMU` (M4 safety boards; split decided 2026-10-24, D-082 item 2):** **`C1-IO` (`4×6 cm`, in the Main Box, never on Board B):** `74AHCT125` 5 V LED buffer, two `HLK-LD2451` UART headers, the immobilizer driver (latching-relay coil drivers only), the ring-supply dead-man `74HC123` (D-072 item 5; required unless the moving alive pixel is used), and the Q-033 indicator inputs (two `PC817` opto-isolated inputs, the M4's own input, D-075 item 4); own fused branch from the protected 12 V (not shared with C2), own connector to the M4 pins; its 5 V comes from the Board A buck; whether it fits beside A and B on the 2nd story is **CONFIRM**. **`C1-IMU` (`3×7 cm` cut to ~`30×40 mm`, own small frame box):** only the M4's own `MPU-6500` (SPI; D-042, D-075 item 6), its LDO (fed from the C1-IO branch through the C1-IMU cable) and 33-100 ohm series resistors on the SPI lines against back-powering; a C1-IMU LDO or cable loss shows as a WHO_AM_I/self-test fault -> UNAVAILABLE. Separation is honest, not independent (D-082 item 2, prototype, no dependent-failure analysis): a C2 fault cannot remove an M4 function; a C1-IMU fault removes only the M4 IMU (cornering UNAVAILABLE on the ring); a C1-IO fault removes its functions together (dark ring / UNAVAILABLE); common causes remain (Board A protected 12 V and buck, star ground, H755 supply/clock/reset, mounting point and loom route, same IMU family).
> 4. **`Board C2` (M7 chassis IMU carrier, `3×7 cm` cut to ~`30×40 mm`):** the M7 chassis IMU only (the on-hand board marked "MPU-6590", chip **CONFIRM**). Separate cable, separate supply branch.
>
> C1-IMU and C2 sit on short rigid `6 mm` brass standoffs, each in its own box bolted to the main frame near the centre of mass and aligned with the bike axes; never on a damped tray and never on the tall 2nd-story columns. Their exact box and position stay with Q-034. **Box sizes (corrected 2026-10-24 from the linked Robolink product pages):** the `DT-0505` is `126×136×48 mm` and the `DT-0508` `126×136×54 mm`, ABS, and neither page states an IP rating or a gasket. Earlier versions of this list gave `~65×50×35`, `65×38×22` (`DT-0505`) and `~83×58×33 mm` (`DT-0508`); those numbers had no source. They are far too large for a ~`30×40 mm` IMU board at a frame point near the centre of mass and their sealing is unknown: the two IMU boxes (C1-IMU, C2) are the same small IP65/IP67 box, product and inner size **CONFIRM** with Q-034 before ordering (also for the small sensor boxes of 2D/3C).
